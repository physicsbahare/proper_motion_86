## Decision

The **early F444W exposure leave-one-out check is reassuring about the existence of a measured displacement**, but the current compact-control STPSF comparison is too small and not externally certified to calibrate a color-dependent systematic. Continue treating 1651050 as a candidate with an apparent two-epoch displacement, not a newly confirmed intrinsic proper-motion detection. A third independent epoch would separate proper motion and parallax more reliably; this check cannot manufacture one.
